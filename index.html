Two decades of microfinance

More than 2 decades of experience providing microfinance facilities to the rural populace. We understand how the rural financial market works, which helps us connect better with clients, and pair credit with credit-plus activities for a better quality of life.

Our story

Home-grown on the coast of Odisha.

G U Financial Services Pvt Ltd (GUFSPL), registered as a Non-Banking Financial Company – Microfinance Institution, is a home-grown and one of the leading organisations in the coastal belt of Odisha. It offers micro loans ranging from ₹12,000 to ₹75,000 under need-based products including Sweet Home for house repairs, renovation or extension, Nirmal for water, sanitation & hygiene, Samrudhi for micro enterprises and Integrated Fish Farming (IFF).

GUFSPL has been providing micro credit services since 2008. Incorporated in 1989 under the Companies Act, 1956, it registered with RBI in 2013 and converted to an NBFC-MFI on 22 August 2018. It is a prominent venture for financial inclusion, aspiring to provide products and services in the micro credit space to promote financial inclusion in Odisha.

GUFSPL provides activity-based products — Unnati for income-generating and agri-allied activities, Sweet Home, Nirmal, Samrudhi, and Uday for Integrated Fish Farming. The company works not only to reach un-served areas, but also for the true development of the households it serves, through continued innovation in products and smooth delivery. Starting in 2008 in Kendrapara district alone, it now operates 52 branches across 16 districts in Odisha and Chhattisgarh.
